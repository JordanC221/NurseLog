# Privacy Policy Draft

This is a draft, not approved legal language. The actual operator, jurisdiction, providers, retention periods, support address, and disclosures require legal review.

NurseLog is an educational and clinical-learning record tool. It is not established as HIPAA compliant and must not be used with identifiable patient information or for patient-specific medical decisions without additional safeguards and review.

Potential collected data includes account and email data, clinical-learning entries, uploaded course materials, study responses, usage metrics, subscription status, and support communications. AI prompts must avoid real patient identifiers and protected health information.
